Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

In short: Hisaab only uses your data to show you your own expenses and who owes you. It isn't sold, shared or used for ads.

What Hisaab is

Hisaab is a personal expense-splitting app. You import your bank statement, split expenses with friends, and track who owes you money.

Information we collect

  • Your Google account basics: when you choose "Continue with Google", we receive your name, email address and Google account ID. We use them only to sign you in and keep your data tied to your account. We never see or store your Google password.
  • What you add to the app: transactions from bank statements you import (dates, descriptions, amounts, balances), expenses you add by hand, the names of people you split with, splits, payments and notes.

Bank statement files are read inside your browser. Only the transactions extracted from them are saved; the original file is never uploaded.

Where it's stored

  • Signed in: your data is stored in a database hosted by Supabase and protected by access rules, so only your signed-in account can read or change it. A copy is also kept in your browser so the app opens quickly and works offline.
  • "Use on this device only": data stays in your browser's local storage and is never uploaded.

The website itself is hosted by Vercel, and sign-in is provided by Google.

How we use it

Only to run the app for you: show your transactions, calculate splits and balances, and sync between your devices. We don't sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it to train models. We don't use tracking or advertising cookies.

Other people's names

Names of the people you split expenses with are only visible to you. They're never contacted by Hisaab. Reminder messages are copied to your clipboard for you to send yourself.

Your choices

  • Export: Backup & settings → Download backup gives you a full copy of your data.
  • Delete: Backup & settings → Erase all data deletes your transactions, people, splits and payments from your account.
  • Delete your account entirely: email us (below) from the Gmail address you sign in with, and we'll delete your account and all stored data within 30 days.
  • Sign out: removes your data from that browser. It stays in your account.

Security

Connections are encrypted (HTTPS), and the database only lets each account access its own data. No system is perfectly secure, so keep regular backups and sign out on shared computers.

Children

Hisaab isn't intended for children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
